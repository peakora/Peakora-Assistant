Your 5-Minute Reset Guide

Five small resets you can do anywhere in five minutes or less. None of them need an account, a card, or a streak to protect. They all live inside the free Peakora Assistant.

1 The Breath Ring

A hard day usually starts in the body before the mind catches up. The breath ring slows that down: one slow 4-2-6 cycle - inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 2, exhale for 6.

  1. Sit or stand somewhere you can be still for a minute.
  2. Open Peakora and tap the breath ring on the Home tab (free, always).
  3. Follow the ring for 10 cycles. Do not force it. Let the exhale be longer than the inhale.
  4. When it ends, notice one thing: your shoulders are usually lower.
Why it works: a longer exhale is what many people find slows a racing heart. It is the fastest physical reset in the app.
2 The Mood Check

Half of managing a day is naming what is actually going on. Peakora's mood check is one honest feeling. No journaling homework, no streaks.

  1. Log how you feel right now - one word or feeling is enough.
  2. That is it. You are done.
  3. Come back tonight if you want: two honest feelings, one day, and the shape of the week shows up in Insights.
Why it works: naming an emotion gives you a little distance from it. Two minutes, no effort, and a real record of your week.
3 One Soundscape, On Purpose

Sound changes how you feel faster than words do. The Soundscape Studio (free) has 8 ambient worlds - bowl, rain, fire, om, wind, binaural, ocean, night - layered and tunable.

  1. For focus: rain or binaural, low volume, headphones or not.
  2. For sleep: ocean or night, dim the screen, pick one track and put the phone down.
  3. For a jittery mind: bowl or om - one layer, nothing else, three minutes.
Why it works: you are not waiting for a mood to arrive. You are choosing the soundtrack your brain gets to process. That is a decision, and it is yours.
4 One Quiet Minute

The hardest reset is the one with nothing in it - and often the most useful. One quiet minute with no goal, no track, no counting.

  1. Set a one-minute timer (or just use your phone's clock).
  2. Sit. Let your eyes rest on something soft. No swiping, no list-running.
  3. When the minute ends, notice what changed. It is usually more than a minute's worth.
Why it works: the pause is the point. You are practicing being with yourself without doing anything - the skill every other reset builds on.
5 One Tiny Win

Momentum is not carried all day - it is rekindled, on purpose. The smallest win today is the thing tomorrow stands on. Write down one thing you will do today, and do it.

  1. Write one thing, not a list. One thing that, if done, makes tomorrow lighter.
  2. Make it embarrassingly small if today is heavy: drink water, step outside, send the text.
  3. Do it. Then give yourself credit - that was the day's reset.
Why it works: deciding beats planning. One small action proves to you that momentum is possible today.
