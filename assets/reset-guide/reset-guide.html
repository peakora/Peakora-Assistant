Your 5-Minute Reset Guide

Five real resets you can do anywhere in five minutes or less. No account, no card, no streaks to protect. Just small, honest things that bring you back to yourself - and every one of them lives inside the free Peakora Assistant.

1 The Breath Ring 1 minute

A hard day usually starts in the body before the mind catches up. The breath ring slows that down: one slow 4-2-6 breathing cycle - inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 2, exhale for 6.

  1. Sit or stand somewhere you can be still for a minute.
  2. Open Peakora and tap the breath ring on the Home tab (free, always).
  3. Follow the ring for 10 cycles. Do not force it. Let the exhale be longer than the inhale.
  4. When the session ends, notice one thing: your shoulders are usually lower.
Why it works: the longer exhale tells your nervous system the threat signal can stand down. It is the fastest physical reset in the whole app.
2 The Mood Check 2 minutes

Half of managing a day is simply naming what is actually going on. Peakora's mood check is one honest feeling, no journaling homework, no streaks.

  1. Log how you feel right now - just one word or feeling.
  2. That is it. You are done.
  3. Come back at the end of the day if you want: two honest feelings, one day, and the shape of the week shows up in Insights.
Why it works: naming an emotion sinks the spike by activating the reasoning part of the brain. Two minutes, no effort, real data for the week.
3 One Soundscape, On Purpose 3-5 minutes

Sound shapes mood faster than words. Peakora's Soundscape Studio (free) has 8 ambient worlds - bowl, rain, fire, om, wind, binaural, ocean, night - layered and tunable.

  1. For focus: rain or binaural, low volume, headphones or not.
  2. For sleep: ocean or night, dim the screen, set one track and put the phone down.
  3. For a jittery mind: bowl or om - one layer, nothing else, three minutes.
Why it works: you are not "relaxing", you are deliberately changing the soundtrack your brain is processing. That is a choice, not a mood you have to wait for.
4 One Quiet Minute 1 minute

The hardest reset is the one with nothing in it - and it is often the most useful. One quiet minute with no goal, no track, no counting.

  1. Set a one-minute timer (or just use the clock on your phone).
  2. Sit. Let your eyes rest on something soft. No phone swiping, no list-running.
  3. When the minute ends, notice what changed - it is usually more than a minute's worth.
Why it works: the pause is the point. You are practicing being with yourself without doing anything - the skill every other reset builds on.
5 One Tiny Win under 5 minutes

Momentum is not sustained - it is rekindled, repeatedly. The smallest win today is the thing tomorrow stands on. Write down one thing you will actually do today, then do it.

  1. Write one thing, not a list. One thing that, if done, makes tomorrow lighter.
  2. Make it embarrassingly small if today is heavy: drink water, step outside, send the text.
  3. Do it. Then give yourself credit - that was the day's reset.
Why it works: deciding beats planning. One small action proves to you that momentum is possible today.
